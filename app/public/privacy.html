LangTutor privacy policy

Last updated: 21 November 2026

LangTutor is a free, open-source Italian learning app. This page explains what it does with your data.

Works without an account

You can use LangTutor completely signed out. Your words, cards and review history are then stored only in your own browser on your own device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Optional Google sign-in

If you choose to sign in with Google, the app receives your Google account's email address and name from Google, only to identify your account and keep your data in sync across your devices. We do not request access to any other Google data (no contacts, Drive, Gmail or calendar).

What is stored when you sign in

Your learning data (your saved words and phrases, flashcards, review history and app settings) is copied to a database hosted by Supabase, tied to your account, so it follows you between devices. Access is restricted to your own account.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, share or give your data to anyone.
  • We do not show ads and we do not use analytics or tracking cookies.
  • We do not use your data for anything other than running the app for you.

Deleting your data

Signing out leaves the data on your device. To have your synced data removed from the server, open an issue on the project's GitHub page and ask for deletion, giving the email you signed in with. You can also export your data from Settings at any time.

Third parties

Sign-in is provided by Google, and synced data is stored by Supabase. The app itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. Each of these handles data under its own privacy policy.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/rezache4/langtutor/issues.
